# Appendix B — The Study Rules, In Detail

## B.1 How to Grade Yourself Without a TA
Ranked by objective usefulness:
1. **Autograders & Test Suites:** `make grade` in 6.1810, Gradescope for 15-445, full test suites in CS144, BusTub, clox, Monkey, 6.5840.
2. **Past Exams, Timed, Closed-Book:** MIT OCW, Berkeley's exam archive (`hkn.eecs.berkeley.edu/exams`), CMU course sites. Sit them under real examination conditions.
3. **Write the Solution for Publication:** If the write-up is vague, so is your understanding.
4. **Code Review from Strangers:** Open a PR against a real open-source project.
5. **Teach It:** A blog post explaining the hardest idea in the block clearly.

---

## B.2 The Weekly Shape (Part-Time: ~20 Hours/Week)
- **Weekday mornings, 90 min before work:** The hardest material (proofs, theory, algorithms). Protected.
- **Weekday evenings, 60 min:** Lectures, reading, Anki.
- **Saturday, 4–6 hrs:** The build block. Systems projects live here.
- **Sunday, 2 hrs:** Psets, writing, weekly review, plan next week.

---

## B.3 Time, Honestly
- MIT counts one "unit" as roughly 1 hr/wk for a 14-week term; a 12-unit subject is ~170 hours.
- The whole program is roughly **6,500–8,000 hours**.
- Below 15 hrs/wk, cut scope rather than extending the timeline beyond 8 years.

---

## B.4 Community — Do Not Skip
- **Recurse Center (`recurse.com`):** Free, self-directed 6- or 12-week retreat (remote or NYC). Highest-value single thing available to a self-taught programmer. Apply after Block 12.
- **Study Partner:** One person on the same path, weekly video call, screen-share psets. Roughly doubles completion rates.
- **Communities:** Papers We Love, OSSU Discord, language Discords (Rust, Zig, Haskell), auditing local university lectures.
