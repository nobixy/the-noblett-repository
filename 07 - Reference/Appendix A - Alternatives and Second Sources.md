## 📐 Extra Math for Specializations
- **Abstract Algebra:** Artin; Harvard Math 122 videos free (for crypto/coding theory)
- **Measure-Theoretic Probability:** Durrett (free)
- **Randomized Algorithms:** Mitzenmacher & Upfal
- **Spectral Graph Theory:** Spielman's notes (free)
- **Category Theory for PL:** Milewski's *Category Theory for Programmers* (free)
- **Logic:** Enderton
- **Global Math Map:** Evan Chen's *Napkin* (free); Courant & Robbins *What Is Mathematics?*
