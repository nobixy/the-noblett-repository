# Courses and Videos

Free YouTube series, Coursera courses (most can be audited for free; with Coursera Plus you also get the graded quizzes and certificates), and other free platforms — mapped to the exact stage or module they support.

**Read this first:**
- **Builds come first.** These are companions, not replacements. A stage is done when its self-check and "Done when" list are done, not when you've finished a playlist.
- **Use the [V protocol](study-protocols.md#v--watch-actively):** pause every 5 minutes and predict, then blank-sheet [R] and practise straight after watching. Aim for about 1 minute of video per 2 minutes of doing.
- **Don't do course projects instead of the curriculum's projects.** Take lectures, quizzes, and practice from courses. Build the curriculum's own projects. (This keeps everything original and on your own machine.)
- **Pick one main companion per stage.** The others are there for when the first explanation doesn't click.

**Key:** ▶️ YouTube (free) · 🆓 free website/course · 🎓 Coursera (audit free; graded parts with Coursera Plus) · 📺 edX (audit free) · 💲 paid

### A daily extra: touch typing (10 minutes, weeks 1–12)

Typing without looking frees your attention for spelling, sentences, and code. It also lets your fingers learn spellings (the same idea as copywork). Use **keybr.com** (it teaches letters a few at a time) for 10 minutes a day until you reach about 35 words per minute with 97% accuracy. Then switch to **monkeytype.com** with its English 1k word list, which drills the same high-frequency words as E01. Your vault also has an open-source project idea about Monkeytype: [OSS Project — Embeddable Typing Test](<02 - Atlas/OSS Project - Embeddable Typing Test (Monkeytype).md>).

## How to fit courses into the week

| Slot | Add |
| :-- | :-- |
| Weekday evenings (English) | One 10–15 minute video or one Khan/Google lesson **twice a week**, followed by its practice |
| Weekday mornings (Math) | Khan practice sets as warm-ups (they're auto-checked — perfect for [I] mixed review) |
| Saturday (build) | One Crash Course CS or Ben Eater video about the system you're building, *before* the build session, as motivation |
| Sunday (review) | One "just for fun" video as a reward after the weekly review |

When a course has graded quizzes (Coursera Plus, Khan unit tests), use them as **extra cold self-checks**. Log the scores in your weekly review.
