## 🚀 Section 1 — do this

Eight sessions (0–7), in order. If a session needs two sittings, that's fine. Log each session.

- [ ] **Session 0 — Placement.** Take the [English placement diagnostic](00-foundations/english/placement.md) and the [M01 diagnostic](00-foundations/math/M01-whole-numbers-and-place-value.md#diagnostic-cold) (cold). If you already know the shell and git, take the [Lab 00 skip check](01-intro-cs-taste/labs/lab-00-machine-setup.md#skip-check). Note where each one says to start.
- [ ] **Session 1 — Measure and begin.** English [E01 Part 1](00-foundations/english/E01-spelling-baseline-and-high-frequency-words.md#part-1--measure-your-baseline-section-1-sessions-13): record the 60-word dictation list on your phone (don't study it). Write your first journal entry.
- [ ] **Session 2 — The terminal.** [Lab 00 Session 1](01-intro-cs-taste/labs/lab-00-machine-setup.md#session-1--the-terminal): the ten commands, paths, tab completion. Then E01 Test B (the free-write).
- [ ] **Session 3 — Your baseline.** E01 Test A (play the earlier recording; write the words) and Test C (proofreading). Write your baseline note. Start `spelling-log.tsv` ([E01 Part 2](00-foundations/english/E01-spelling-baseline-and-high-frequency-words.md#part-2--start-your-error-log-section-1-session-4)).
- [ ] **Session 4 — Place value.** [M01](00-foundations/math/M01-whole-numbers-and-place-value.md) Parts 1–2. Then 10 words with Look–Say–Cover–Write–Check ([E01 Part 3](00-foundations/english/E01-spelling-baseline-and-high-frequency-words.md#part-3--how-to-learn-a-spelling-for-good)).
- [ ] **Session 5 — Your workbench.** [Lab 00 Session 3](01-intro-cs-taste/labs/lab-00-machine-setup.md#session-3--git-and-your-workbench): git and `~/workbench/`. Commit your baseline note and spelling log — your first commit.
- [ ] **Session 6 (build) — Count in other bases.** [Base Workshop Milestone 1](00-foundations/math/projects/base-workshop/spec.md#milestone-1--the-counting-board-m01-paper): the paper counting board. Then your first **Franklin copywork** session ([study-protocols C](study-protocols.md#c--franklin-copywork)) on a Simple English Wikipedia passage about computers.
- [ ] **Session 7 — Section Review.** [Section Review](<04 - System/Section Review Template.md>): flashcards set up (Anki, or a paper Leitner box), one blank-sheet recall [R] of the section, plan Section 2 (E01 Part 4 and M01 Parts 3–5; Lab 00 Session 2).

**After Section 1:** follow **[The First Sections](00-foundations/first-sections.md)** (Sections 2–12: targets, videos, fun picks, badges), then the checklist below, using the session structure in [study-protocols](study-protocols.md#the-session-loop): rotate math, English and build sessions, and close each section with a Section Review. Start Module 01 in Section 3.

## Operating rules

1. **Done means done.** A stage or project is complete when its "Done when" list is true and its deliverable exists.
2. **One build project at a time.** A *build project* is any project spec (foundation, module, or Module 12). Stage lessons, labs and units may run alongside it, and up to two modules may be open at once (for example 02 and 03, or 03 and 04), but don't start a second project until the first is done (or deliberately paused with a note).
3. **Minimum session when a session goes wrong:** flashcards plus one copywork sentence, and a two-line log. Small sessions keep the chain alive.
4. **Retrieve, don't reread.** Every session ends with a blank sheet [R].
5. **The stuck rule.** Three honest attempts, then a stuck note, a break, and return [D].
6. **Fell off the routine?** Restart with one normal session. No catch-up binges; no guilt.
7. **Scope control.** Stretch goals are optional. Finish the core of a project before adding to it.
8. **Evidence over feeling.** Tests, measurements, and other people's reactions beat "I think I get it."
9. **Privacy.** Personal financial, health, identity, and relationship data never goes in this repository.
10. **Change the system deliberately.** If a rule or plan isn't working after a fair trial, write a [Decision Record](<04 - System/Decision Record.md>). Don't rebuild on impulse.
