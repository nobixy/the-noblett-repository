## 🚀 Week 1 — do this

Each day is about 60–90 minutes. Do them in order; if a day takes two days, that's fine. Log each day.

- [ ] **Day 1 — Measure and begin.** English [E01 Part 1](00-foundations/english/E01-spelling-baseline-and-high-frequency-words.md#part-1--measure-your-baseline-week-1-days-13): record the 60-word dictation list on your phone (don't study it). Math [M01 diagnostic](00-foundations/math/M01-whole-numbers-and-place-value.md#diagnostic-cold-15-minutes) (15 minutes, cold). Write today's journal entry.
- [ ] **Day 2 — The terminal.** [Lab 00 Session 1](01-intro-cs-taste/labs/lab-00-machine-setup.md#session-1--the-terminal-2-hours): the ten commands, paths, tab completion. Then E01 Test B (15-minute free-write).
- [ ] **Day 3 — Your baseline.** E01 Test A (play yesterday's recording; write the words) and Test C (proofreading). Write your baseline note. Start `spelling-log.tsv` ([E01 Part 2](00-foundations/english/E01-spelling-baseline-and-high-frequency-words.md#part-2--start-your-error-log-week-1-day-4)).
- [ ] **Day 4 — Place value.** [M01](00-foundations/math/M01-whole-numbers-and-place-value.md) Parts 1–2. Then 10 words with Look–Say–Cover–Write–Check ([E01 Part 3](00-foundations/english/E01-spelling-baseline-and-high-frequency-words.md#part-3--how-to-learn-a-spelling-for-good)).
- [ ] **Day 5 — Your workbench.** [Lab 00 Session 3](01-intro-cs-taste/labs/lab-00-machine-setup.md#session-3--git-and-your-workbench-2-hours): git and `~/workbench/`. Commit your baseline note and spelling log — your first commit.
- [ ] **Day 6 (Saturday build) — Count in other bases.** [Base Workshop Milestone 1](00-foundations/math/projects/base-workshop/spec.md#milestone-1--the-counting-board-m01-paper): the paper counting board. Then your first **Franklin copywork** session ([study-protocols C](study-protocols.md#c--franklin-copywork)) on a Simple English Wikipedia passage about computers.
- [ ] **Day 7 (Sunday) — Review.** [Weekly Review](<04 - System/Weekly Review Template.md>): flashcards set up (Anki, or a paper Leitner box), one blank-sheet recall [R] of the week, plan week 2 (E01 Part 4 and M01 Parts 3–5; Lab 00 Session 2).

**After Week 1:** follow **[The First Twelve Weeks](00-foundations/first-twelve-weeks.md)** (week-by-week targets, videos, fun picks, badges), then the checklist below, using the daily shape in the [README](README.md#the-daily-and-weekly-shape-about-20-hours-a-week): math mornings, English evenings, builds on Saturdays, review on Sundays. Start Module 01 in week 3.

## Operating rules

1. **Done means done.** A stage or project is complete when its "Done when" list is true and its deliverable exists.
2. **One build project at a time.** English and math run alongside; don't start a second build project until the first is done (or deliberately paused with a note).
3. **Daily minimum on bad days:** flashcards plus one copywork sentence, and a two-line log. Small days keep the chain alive.
4. **Retrieve, don't reread.** Every session ends with a blank sheet [R].
5. **The 90-minute stuck rule.** Stuck note, break, return [D].
6. **Missed a week?** Restart with one normal day. No catch-up binges; no guilt.
7. **Scope control.** Stretch goals are optional. Finish the core of a project before adding to it.
8. **Evidence over feeling.** Tests, measurements, and other people's reactions beat "I think I get it."
9. **Privacy.** Personal financial, health, identity, and relationship data never goes in this repository.
10. **Change the system deliberately.** If a rule or plan isn't working after a fair trial, write a [Decision Record](<04 - System/Decision Record.md>). Don't rebuild on impulse.
