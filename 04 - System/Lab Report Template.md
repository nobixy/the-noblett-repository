# Lab Report: {{lab}}

*A lab report answers one question with evidence. Keep sentences short. Every claim points to a number, a table, a plot, or a photo.*

## 1. Question
One sentence. What did you want to find out? *Example: "How does the time to sort a list grow as the list gets longer?"*

## 2. Prediction
Before measuring: what do you expect, and why? (This is not graded on being right. It is graded on having a reason.)

## 3. Setup
What you used (hardware, software versions, parts) and what you did, step by step, so someone else could repeat it. Include a photo or diagram for anything physical.

## 4. Results
Tables and plots. Label every axis and give units. Then 2–4 sentences saying what the data shows, without explaining it yet.

## 5. Analysis
Why did you get these results? Did they match your prediction? If not, which part of your reasoning was wrong? [W]

## 6. Sources of error
What could have made the numbers wrong? How big could the effect be?

## 7. Conclusion
Answer the question from section 1 in one or two sentences.

## 8. What I would test next
One follow-up experiment.
