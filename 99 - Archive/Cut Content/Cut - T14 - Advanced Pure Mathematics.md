## 🎯 Why This Track Matters
Pushing the boundary in Quantum Computing, Elliptic Curve Cryptography, Topological Data Analysis, and advanced Robotics (Lie Algebras) requires mathematical maturity far beyond standard engineering calculus. This track builds the pure mathematics infrastructure necessary for bleeding-edge theoretical computer science.

## 📖 Primary Syllabus & Core Content
- [ ] **Abstract Algebra:** Groups, Rings, Fields, Galois Theory.
- [ ] **Point-Set Topology:** Open/Closed sets, Continuity, Compactness, Connectedness, Separation Axioms.
- [ ] **Algebraic Topology:** The Fundamental Group, Covering Spaces, Simplicial Homology.
- [ ] **Differential Geometry:** Smooth Manifolds, Tangent Spaces, Differential Forms, Lie Groups, and Lie Algebras.

## 🛠️ Build Requirement
Instead of software builds, this track requires rigorous, mathematician-grade proofs for hundreds of textbook exercises. 
- You must maintain a LaTeX portfolio of proofs for the core theorems of Algebra, Topology, and Differential Geometry.
- You must formally present (via the Feynman method) the connection between Lie Algebras and robotics state estimation, or between Galois Theory and cryptographic fields.

## 🏁 Mastery Criteria & Assessments
> [!IMPORTANT]
> All proofs terminate with a Q.E.D. tombstone, completely satisfy the hypotheses, and demonstrate absolute rigorous logic without hand-waving.
