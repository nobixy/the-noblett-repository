# Writing Deliverables Hub
*Writing is part of every English session (copywork [C] and the [fun write](<../00-foundations/english/writing-prompts.md>)), and every project's README, design doc and write-up is writing practice. The skills are taught in the [English track](<../00-foundations/english/overview.md>) (E01–E10).*

---

> [!INFO] Writing Rules
> - Use [[500-Word Essay Template]] for longer essays.
> - Publish technical blog posts to an external site/blog (optional).

## Writing deliverables in the curriculum
- **English E08–E10:** paragraphs, technical description, instructions, bug reports, arguments, design docs, and recorded explainers ([Explain-a-System](<../00-foundations/english/projects/explain-a-system/spec.md>), [First Design Doc](<../00-foundations/english/projects/first-design-doc/spec.md>)).
- **Every module project:** a README, a design note or design doc, and the write-up named in its spec (the T protocol in [study-protocols](<../study-protocols.md>)).
- **Paper summaries:** optional, from the [[Paper Reading Hub]].
- **Capstone:** a proposal (design doc), review notes, a final report (5,000–8,000 words) and a recorded talk ([the capstone spec](<../13-capstone/projects/the-whole-stack/spec.md>)).

*Historical note: the v1 year-by-year writing targets and the "500 words a day" habit were removed ([DR-011](<../04 - System/DR-011 - Sectioned Curriculum and Frontmatter Schema.md>)).*

## Writing Books (suggested reading order)
1. **Williams & Bizup**, *Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace* — The single most useful book on English prose. Do the exercises. It rewards rereading.
2. **Pinker**, *The Sense of Style* — Read chapter 3 ("The Curse of Knowledge") twice.
3. **Stanford Online / Kristin Sainani**, *Writing in the Sciences* (free) — Clutter, verbs, paragraphs, manuscript structure, peer review.
4. **Zobel**, *Writing for Computer Science, 3rd ed.* — Algorithms, experiments, honest graphs.
5. **Booth, Colomb & Williams**, *The Craft of Research, 5th ed.* — Closest thing to a research-methods course in book form.
6. **Schimel**, *Writing Science* — Story structure for technical papers. Pair with Knuth, Larrabee & Roberts, *Mathematical Writing* (free PDF).
7. **Zinsser**, *On Writing Well* — Voice and ruthlessness about clutter.
8. **Graff & Birkenstein**, *They Say / I Say* — Templates for entering an academic conversation.
9. **McPhee**, *Draft No. 4* — The craft of narrative structure and non-fiction.
