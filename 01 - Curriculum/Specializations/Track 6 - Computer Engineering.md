# Track 6: Computer Engineering (Deep Hardware)

> [!INFO] Specialization Structure
> Each specialization track consists of **two courses** plus a **substantial build deliverable**.
> In Part 3 of the curriculum: Pick **two tracks** across Years 4 & 5 (*"Two deep beats six shallow"*).

---

## 🎓 Course 1
Onur Mutlu, Graduate Computer Architecture, ETH Zürich (free; out-of-order execution, memory systems, cache coherence) with Hennessy & Patterson, Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach, 6th ed.

---

## 🎓 Course 2
MIT 6.003 / 6.3000 Signals and Systems (OCW, Alan Oppenheim) — required for anything touching DSP, communications, or control systems

---

## 🛠️ The Track Build Deliverable
Extend your Block 14 RISC-V core with branch prediction, a multi-level cache hierarchy, and out-of-order execution. Characterize performance in gem5. Then tape out an ASIC via Tiny Tapeout or Efabless.

---

## 🚀 Extensions & Advanced Material
MIT 6.002 Circuits (Agarwal & Lang) + Horowitz & Hill; Lee & Seshia, Introduction to Embedded Systems (free) with bare-metal STM32/RP2040 firmware; Weste & Harris, CMOS VLSI Design with OpenLane/SkyWater flow.

---

## 📚 Key Reference Texts
Hennessy & Patterson, CA:AQA 6e; Horowitz & Hill, The Art of Electronics; Weste & Harris, CMOS VLSI Design.
