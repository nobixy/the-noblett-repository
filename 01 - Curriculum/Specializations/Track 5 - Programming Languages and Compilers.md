# Track 5: Programming Languages and Compilers

> [!INFO] Specialization Structure
> Each specialization track consists of **two courses** plus a **substantial build deliverable**.
> In Part 3 of the curriculum: Pick **two tracks** across Years 4 & 5 (*"Two deep beats six shallow"*).

---

## 🎓 Course 1
Dan Grossman, Programming Languages (UW, Coursera, free — SML, Racket, Ruby) with Benjamin Pierce, Types and Programming Languages (TAPL)

---

## 🎓 Course 2
Cornell CS 6120 Advanced Compilers (Adrian Sampson — designed to be self-guided) with Cooper & Torczon, Engineering a Compiler, 3rd ed.

---

## 🛠️ The Track Build Deliverable
An optimizing compiler for a small language targeting real x86-64 or RISC-V with SSA form, register allocation, and three optimization passes. Then Pierce et al., Software Foundations Vol. 1–2 (free, Coq/Rocq).

---

## 🚀 Extensions & Advanced Material
OPLSS summer school lectures (free); Adam Chlipala, Certified Programming with Dependent Types (free); LLVM backend pass development.

---

## 📚 Key Reference Texts
Pierce, TAPL; Cooper & Torczon, Engineering a Compiler; Pierce et al., Software Foundations.
