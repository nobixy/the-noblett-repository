# Track 2: Systems and Performance

> [!INFO] Specialization Structure
> Each specialization track consists of **two courses** plus a **substantial build deliverable**.
> In Part 3 of the curriculum: Pick **two tracks** across Years 4 & 5 (*"Two deep beats six shallow"*).

---

## 🎓 Course 1
MIT 6.172 Performance Engineering of Software Systems (OCW, Leiserson) — cache-aware algorithms, vectorization, parallelism, profiling

---

## 🎓 Course 2
CMU 15-721 Advanced Database Systems (Andy Pavlo) OR Herlihy & Shavit, The Art of Multiprocessor Programming worked as a course

---

## 🛠️ The Track Build Deliverable
Merged patches to the Linux Kernel, PostgreSQL, LLVM, or a major language runtime (e.g. V8, Go runtime). The most legible credential in systems.

---

## 🚀 Extensions & Advanced Material
Brendan Gregg, Systems Performance 2e; Agner Fog's optimization manuals (free online); Linux kernel mailing list (LKML) exploration.

---

## 📚 Key Reference Texts
Gregg, Systems Performance; Agner Fog's Manuals; Herlihy & Shavit, Multiprocessor Programming.
