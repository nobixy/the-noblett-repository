# Track 1: AI and Machine Learning

> [!INFO] Specialization Structure
> Each specialization track consists of **two courses** plus a **substantial build deliverable**.
> In Part 3 of the curriculum: Pick **two tracks** across Years 4 & 5 (*"Two deep beats six shallow"*).

---

## 🎓 Course 1
Stanford CS229 (Andrew Ng — real course) with Bishop, Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts (bishopbook.com)

---

## 🎓 Course 2
Andrej Karpathy, Neural Networks: Zero to Hero (free) → then Stanford CS231n (Vision) or CS224n (NLP)

---

## 🛠️ The Track Build Deliverable
A transformer, from scratch, that trains to a reasonable loss. Then CMU 10-414 Deep Learning Systems (dlsyscourse.org): build a complete PyTorch-like framework from scratch.

---

## 🚀 Extensions & Advanced Material
Sutton & Barto + Berkeley CS285 for Deep Reinforcement Learning; MIT 6.5940 (Song Han) for TinyML and Efficient ML.

---

## 📚 Key Reference Texts
Bishop, Deep Learning; Sutton & Barto, Reinforcement Learning 2e; Goodfellow et al., Deep Learning.
