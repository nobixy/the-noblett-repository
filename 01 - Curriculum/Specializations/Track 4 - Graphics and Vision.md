# Track 4: Graphics and Vision

> [!INFO] Specialization Structure
> Each specialization track consists of **two courses** plus a **substantial build deliverable**.
> In Part 3 of the curriculum: Pick **two tracks** across Years 4 & 5 (*"Two deep beats six shallow"*).

---

## 🎓 Course 1
GAMES101: Introduction to Computer Graphics (Lingqi Yan; free, English lectures, excellent homework assignments)

---

## 🎓 Course 2
CMU 15-462 or Berkeley CS184, with Pharr, Jakob & Humphreys, Physically Based Rendering: From Theory to Implementation, 4th ed. (free at pbr-book.org)

---

## 🛠️ The Track Build Deliverable
A physically-based Monte Carlo path tracer with importance sampling and a Bounding Volume Hierarchy (BVH); then a real-time renderer implemented in Vulkan or WebGPU.

---

## 🚀 Extensions & Advanced Material
Stanford CS231n; Richard Szeliski, Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications 2e (free); Hartley & Zisserman, Multiple View Geometry.

---

## 📚 Key Reference Texts
Pharr, Jakob & Humphreys, PBRT 4e; Szeliski, Computer Vision 2e.
