---

## 🎯 Why This Block Matters
Where you learn what a proof actually is. The hardest block for most people and the one that permanently transforms your rigorous analytical thinking.

---

## 📖 Primary Syllabus & Core Content
- [ ] The Real Numbers: Axiom of completeness, inf/sup, cardinality, Cantor's theorem
- [ ] Sequences and Series: Convergence, Cauchy sequences, Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem
- [ ] Basic Topology of R: Open/closed sets, compact sets, connected sets
- [ ] Functional Limits and Continuity: Continuous functions, Intermediate Value Theorem, Extreme Value Theorem, uniform continuity
- [ ] The Derivative: Mean Value Theorem, continuous non-differentiable functions
- [ ] Riemann Integration: Riemann integrability, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- [ ] Sequences and Series of Functions: Pointwise vs uniform convergence, Weierstrass M-test

---

## 🛠️ Build Requirement
Work through Abbott cover-to-cover and write out every single proof without consulting solution guides.

## 🏁 Done When
