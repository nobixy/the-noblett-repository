## 🎯 Why This Block Matters
Proofs, induction, counting, graphs, number theory, recurrences. The load-bearing mathematics for algorithms, complexity, and distributed systems.

## 📖 Primary Syllabus & Core Content
- [ ] Part I: Proofs (well ordering principle, propositional logic, predicate logic, induction, state machines)
- [ ] Part II: Structures (number theory, RSA cryptography, directed graphs, DAGs, relations, partial orders)
- [ ] Part III: Counting (sums, asymptotics, Big-O, recurrences, generating functions, cardinality, infinite sets)
- [ ] Part IV: Probability (events, conditional probability, random variables, expectation, variance, deviation bounds)
- [ ] Companion: Finish Velleman chapters 4–7 in the first month; read What Is Mathematics? chapters 3–5
