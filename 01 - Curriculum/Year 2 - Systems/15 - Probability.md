## 🎯 Why This Block Matters
Probabilistic reasoning is mandatory for randomized algorithms, machine learning, systems performance, and statistical inference.

## 📖 Primary Syllabus & Core Content
- [ ] Sample space, conditioning, Bayes' rule, independence
- [ ] Discrete and continuous random variables, PDFs, CDFs, PMFs
- [ ] Expectation, variance, covariance, conditional expectation
- [ ] Transform methods and moment-generating functions
- [ ] Bernoulli and Poisson processes
- [ ] Markov chains (discrete-time, transition probabilities, steady-state distributions)
- [ ] Laws of Large Numbers and Central Limit Theorem
