## 📖 Primary Syllabus & Core Content
- [ ] Part I: Vectors and Matrices (dot/cross products, planes, lines, linear systems)
- [ ] Part II: Partial Derivatives (tangent planes, min/max, Lagrange multipliers, gradients)
- [ ] Part III: Double and Triple Integrals (polar, cylindrical, spherical coordinates, Jacobians)
- [ ] Part IV: Vector Calculus & Field Theory (line integrals, gradient fields, Green's theorem, divergence, curl, Stokes' theorem)
