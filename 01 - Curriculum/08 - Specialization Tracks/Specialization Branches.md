# Specialization Branches

Deep elective domains. Specialization A opens at Block 26, after the 💼5 courses ([[DR-001 - Program Scope, Phases, and Timeline|DR-001]]). Two tracks belong to the Program; the rest are Lifelong Continuation. Since [[DR-004 - Content Overhaul|DR-004]] every track names the real, current, free courses that teach it (see each note's 🌐 Real Courses section).

## Computer Science
- [[T01 - Deep AI and Machine Learning|Track 1 — AI and Machine Learning]]: ML systems and LLMs from scratch (CS229, CMU DLSys, Stanford CS336).
- [[T02 - Advanced Systems and Performance|Track 2 — Systems and Performance]]: performance engineering, database internals, Rust systems (absorbed former Track 8).
- [[T03 - Advanced Security and Cryptography|Track 3 — Security and Cryptography]]: applied crypto and systems security. See also [[B27 - Intensive Cryptopals|Block 27]].
- [[T05 - Advanced Programming Languages and Compilers|Track 5 — PL, Compilers and Verification]]: semantics, types, optimizing compilers, TLA+/Alloy (absorbed former Track 13).
- [[T10 - Full-Stack and Product Engineering|Track 10 — Full-Stack and Product Engineering]]: shipping real web products.

## Computer Engineering
- [[T06 - Advanced Computer Engineering|Track 6 — Computer Engineering]]: advanced architecture, FPGA, open-source ASIC flow.
- [[T07 - TinyML and Edge AI|Track 7 — TinyML and Edge AI]]: efficient deep learning on microcontrollers.
- [[T04 - Advanced Graphics and Vision|Track 4 — Graphics and Vision]]: rendering, GPUs, computer vision.

## Electrical Engineering
- [[T11 - Signal Processing and Communications|Track 11 — Signal Processing, Communications and Electromagnetics]]: DSP, digital comms, SDR, EM (new in DR-004).
- [[T09 - Autonomous Robotics|Track 9 — Robotics, Control and Cyber-Physical Systems]]: estimation, control, planning, hardware-in-the-loop (absorbed former Track 9).
- [[T08 - Quantum Information and Computing|Track 8 — Quantum Information and Computing]]: quantum algorithms and error correction.

## Removed by DR-004
- Computational Biology (former Track 12) and Advanced Pure Mathematics (former Track 14): outside EECS. Archived in `99 - Archive/Cut Content/`.
