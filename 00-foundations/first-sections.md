# The First Sections (1–12)

**A section-by-section plan for the start, when the habit is still forming.** It tells you exactly what to aim for in each of the first twelve sections in English, math, and your build, which video or course to pair with it, and one fun thing to do. After Section 12 you'll know your own pace, and the stage files and [Start Here](<../00 - Start Here.md>) take over.

**Rules for this plan:**
- It's a **target, not a contract**. A section is done when its targets are done, however many sessions that takes. Never skip a stage's self-check to keep up.
- **Minimum session** (when a session goes wrong): flashcards plus one copywork sentence, plus a two-line log. That still counts.
- Every section ends with the [Section Review](<../04 - System/Section Review Template.md>).

**The session shape** (canonical version: [study-protocols § The session loop](../study-protocols.md#the-session-loop)):

| Session | What |
| :-- | :-- |
| Math | Math stage lesson and practice |
| English | English stage lesson, copywork [C], and a fun write ([prompts](english/writing-prompts.md)) in most English sessions |
| Any session | Touch typing (keybr.com) and flashcards as a warm-up or cool-down |
| Build | Labs and projects (the "Build" row in each section) |
| End of section | Section Review; one "just for fun" video; plan the next section |

Companions come from each track's and module's **Video course** section ([English](english/overview.md#video-course) · [Math](math/overview.md#video-course); how to use them: [study-protocols § Using video courses](../study-protocols.md#using-video-courses)). Use them with the [V protocol](../study-protocols.md#v--watch-actively): pause, predict, then recall and practise.

## Touch typing (Sections 1–12)

Typing without looking frees your attention for spelling, sentences and code, and lets your fingers learn spellings (the same idea as copywork). Use **keybr.com** (it teaches letters a few at a time) as a short warm-up each session until you reach about 35 words per minute with 97% accuracy. Then switch to **monkeytype.com** with its English 1k word list, which drills the same high-frequency words as E01. Your vault also has an open-source project idea about Monkeytype: [OSS Project — Embeddable Typing Test](<../02 - Atlas/OSS Project - Embeddable Typing Test (Monkeytype).md>).

## After section 12: a three-question check-in

Answer these in your Section Review:
1. **Pace:** which track ran ahead, and which fell behind? Should you rebalance how many math, English and build sessions you do?
2. **Methods:** which protocols ([R F W S I C D T V](../study-protocols.md)) actually helped? Which did you skip? Try the skipped one properly for two sections before dropping it.
3. **Fun:** which prompts, games, and videos did you look forward to? Do more of those — enjoyment is fuel.

Then carry on with the stage files and the [Start Here](<../00 - Start Here.md>) checklist: E05–E06 and M04–M05 alongside Relay, Burrow Jr., and Pagelet.
