# 00 — Foundations

Two daily tracks that run underneath everything else in this curriculum:

| Track | Starts at | Ends at | Daily slot | Hours |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | --: |
| [English](english/overview.md) | spelling your first word list | design documents, lab reports, recorded demos | weekday evenings, 45–60 min | ~220 |
| [Math](math/overview.md) | whole numbers and place value | exponentials, logarithms, sums | weekday mornings, 60–90 min | ~315 |

**Why both, and why from the very bottom:** problem-solving needs math you trust, and engineering needs writing and speaking people can follow. Gaps at the bottom cause trouble at every level above. So both tracks start from absolute basics, explain the reason for every rule, and connect each stage to something you'll build.

**How they connect to the build modules:** every module overview lists the English and math stages it needs. [01 Intro CS Taste](../01-intro-cs-taste/overview.md) starts after **E01** and **M01** (about week 3). After the tracks finish, both stay as short daily habits (spelling review, copywork, mixed math review), and every project's deliverable becomes your main writing practice.

**Study methods:** both tracks use the protocols in [study-protocols.md](../study-protocols.md) every day — retrieval, Feynman, why-ladders, subgoal labels, interleaving, spacing, Franklin copywork, diffuse breaks, and write-ups.
