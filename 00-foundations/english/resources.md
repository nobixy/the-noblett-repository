# Foundations: English — Resources

*Part of [Foundations: English](overview.md). Pointers only: the stage files are the course.*

## Video course

*Companions, not replacements: each stage's lessons come first. Watch with the [V protocol](../../study-protocols.md#v--watch-actively): after each video, write the rule from memory with your own example, then do the exercises. How courses fit your sessions: [study-protocols § Using video courses](../../study-protocols.md#using-video-courses).*

**Primary — Khan Academy Grammar**, Khan Academy. Free: [Grammar video playlist on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6CQ7apI_8PjSBN8BxukW5Z76k8lRMQEf) · [Grammar course with practice exercises](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/grammar).
- **Why it fits:** it follows almost the same path as E04–E07: parts of speech, then punctuation, then syntax (sentences, clauses, agreement, fragments, run-ons). Each idea gets its own short video, so you can watch exactly the rule you are on, and the course site adds auto-checked exercises for [R] and [I]. It explains *why* a rule exists, which matches the [W] why-ladders this track uses.

**Alternate — Writing in the Sciences**, Stanford Online (Dr. Kristin Sainani), on Coursera. Listed as free on its course page: [Writing in the Sciences](https://www.coursera.org/learn/sciwrite).
- **Why:** the best free course on *clear* writing: cutting clutter, strong verbs, active voice, punctuation for flow, and paragraphs. That is E08's subject, and the revising habits carry into E09–E10. Its examples come from science papers, but the rules are the ones technical writing uses. Take Modules 1–4; Modules 5–8 are about publishing research papers and can be skipped.

**Gaps:**
- **E01–E03 (spelling):** no free video course teaches English spelling for adults well. Spelling is learned by the stage routines (spelling log, rules, copywork, typing), so keep the stage files' practice tools; the short videos in each stage's **Watch, practise, and write** list are optional.
- **E09–E10:** the best free courses here are text, not video: Google's [Technical Writing One](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one) (E09) and [Technical Writing Two](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/two) (E10). Use them as the main companion for those stages. For demos and talks, Simon Peyton Jones's [How to Give a Great Research Talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ot_McoYlwUo) is the pick (also used in [Module 13](../../13-capstone/resources.md#video-course)).
