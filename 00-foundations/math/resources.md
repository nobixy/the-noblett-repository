# Foundations: Math — Resources

*Part of [Foundations: Math](overview.md). Pointers only: the stage files are the course. Books and practice sites are listed in the overview's [free resources](overview.md#free-resources-pointers-only).*

## Video course

*Companions, not replacements: each stage's lessons come first. Watch with the [V protocol](../../study-protocols.md#v--watch-actively) (pause, predict, then blank-sheet [R]) and do more than you watch. How courses fit your sessions: [study-protocols § Using video courses](../../study-protocols.md#using-video-courses).*

**Primary — Khan Academy math courses**: [Arithmetic](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic) → [Pre-algebra](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/pre-algebra) → [Algebra 1](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra) → [High school geometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/geometry) → [Algebra 2](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra2). Free.
- **Why it fits:** it is the only free course that covers the whole track, from place value to logarithms, in one consistent style. Every short video sits next to **auto-checked practice and unit tests**, which suit the [I] mixed review and cold checks this track runs on. Its unit tests let you skip what you already know, the same way each stage's diagnostic works.

**Alternate — Math Antics** (YouTube). Free: [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@mathantics) · [mathantics.com](https://www.mathantics.com/) (lessons sorted by topic).
- **Why:** slower, friendlier and funnier, with no practice drill attached. Use it when a Khan video moves too fast, especially in M01–M07. It is strongest on arithmetic, fractions, decimals and percents, and stops before logarithms.
