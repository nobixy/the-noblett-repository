# The First Twelve Weeks

**A week-by-week plan for the start, when the habit is still forming.** It tells you exactly what to aim for each week in English, math, and your build, which video or course to pair with it, and one fun thing to do. After week 12 you'll know your own pace, and the stage files and [Start Here](<../00 - Start Here.md>) take over.

**Rules for this plan:**
- It's a **target, not a contract**. If a week takes two, that's fine. Slide everything later; never skip a stage's self-check to keep up.
- **Minimum day** (on bad days): flashcards plus one copywork sentence, plus a two-line log. That still counts as a streak day.
- Every week ends with the [Weekly Review](<../04 - System/Weekly Review Template.md>) on Sunday.

**The daily shape (about 20 hours a week):**

| When | What | Time |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Weekday morning | Math stage lesson and practice | 60–90 min |
| Weekday evening | English stage lesson, copywork [C], a fun write ([prompts](english/writing-prompts.md)) on at least 4 days | 45–60 min |
| Every day | Touch typing (keybr.com) 10 min · flashcards 10–20 min | 20–30 min |
| Saturday | Build block (labs and projects) | 4–6 h |
| Sunday | Weekly review; one "just for fun" video; plan next week | 1–2 h |

Companions come from [courses-and-videos.md](../courses-and-videos.md). Use them with the [V protocol](../study-protocols.md#v--watch-actively): pause, predict, then recall and practise.

## After week 12: a three-question check-in

Answer these in your weekly review:
1. **Pace:** which track ran ahead, and which fell behind? Should you rebalance the morning and evening time?
2. **Methods:** which protocols ([R F W S I C D T V](../study-protocols.md)) actually helped? Which did you skip? Try the skipped one properly for two weeks before dropping it.
3. **Fun:** which prompts, games, and videos did you look forward to? Do more of those — enjoyment is fuel.

Then carry on with the stage files and the [Start Here](<../00 - Start Here.md>) checklist: E05–E06 and M04–M05 alongside Relay, Burrow Jr., and Pagelet.
